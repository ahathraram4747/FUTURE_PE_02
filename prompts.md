# UGC Ad Prompt System

## Master Prompt

Act as an experienced UGC content marketer for a skincare brand.

Create authentic, engaging, short-form UGC advertisement content for:

Brand: GlowNest Skincare
Product: GlowNest Hydrating Face Serum
Target Audience: Young adults interested in simple skincare routines.

The content should:
- Start with a strong attention-grabbing hook.
- Identify a relatable skincare problem.
- Introduce the product as a simple solution.
- Explain the product benefit clearly without making unrealistic claims.
- Use a natural, conversational UGC tone.
- Include a clear call-to-action.
- Avoid overly promotional or robotic language.

Generate:
1. 10 different hooks
2. 3 UGC-style ad scripts
3. 3 captions
4. 5 calls-to-action
5. Platform adaptations for Instagram Reels, Facebook, and YouTube Shorts.

Keep the content concise, authentic, easy to understand, and suitable for short-form video marketing.

## Prompt Objective

This prompt is designed to generate consistent UGC-style marketing content while adapting the message to different social media platforms.
